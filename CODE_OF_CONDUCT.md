# Code of Conduct

Orchestra follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion on the work.
Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information are
not accepted in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, [@lsy041015](https://github.com/lsy041015),
on GitHub. For something you do not want to raise in public, use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature.

The maintainer reviews every report, keeps the reporter's identity private, and
may edit or remove content or block contributors who break this code.

This project is a fork of [Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers),
but the Superpowers and Prime Radiant teams do not moderate it. Send reports
about Orchestra here, not to them.
